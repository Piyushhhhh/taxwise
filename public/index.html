Annual Disclosure — Schedule FA
Even if you sold nothing, disclose every year
₹10L penalty
File ITR-2 (not ITR-1) every year you hold RSUs. In Schedule FA, disclose: broker name, account number, peak value, closing value, income earned. Covers unvested grants too once allocated to your account.

The Black Money Act 2015 penalties for non-disclosure are severe — ₹10 lakh minimum, no matter how small the holding.

FEMA Notification 263 permits employer-granted foreign shares
Permitted
You can hold US company shares received through employment without any LRS limits. This includes unvested and vested shares.

✓ Holding unvested/vested employer shares
✓ Reinvesting sale proceeds in another permissible asset

⚠️
TDS Gap — Most common surprise
Employer may not have deducted TDS correctly
Check your Form 16
Many MNC payroll systems either don't include perquisite TDS at all, or use the wrong exchange rate. This creates a surprise tax liability when you file ITR.

1
Open your payslip for the month RSUs vested
2
Look for "Perquisite" or "ESOP/RSU" line in earnings and a matching TDS deduction
3
If no perquisite TDS was deducted, you must pay advance tax yourself by the next installment
4
Check Form 16 Part B — "Value of Perquisites u/s 17(2)" must show the vest amount

FBAR — Your Indian accounts trigger US filing
Indian savings, FD, demat >$10k at any point = must file
$100k penalty
FinCEN Form 114 (FBAR) due by April 15 (auto-extended to Oct 15). Counts: savings accounts, FDs, demat accounts, PPF, EPF, NPS.

Willful non-filing penalty: $100,000 or 50% of account balance per year. Non-willful: $10,000/year.
📋Also file Form 8938 (FATCA) if foreign assets >$50k single or $100k married.
🌴
California Source Tax Trap
CA taxes RSU income based on where you were when shares were granted
Hidden liability
Moved from California to Texas/Washington after your RSU grant? California still taxes a portion of your vesting income — calculated by the % of days worked in CA between grant and vest.

Moving to a no-tax state does not eliminate this. File Form 3840 (Multi-State RSU Allocation) in the year of vest if you left CA.

Unvested RSUs — Check your award agreement
Company policy varies wildly — most people never read this clause
Check now
Unvested shares are a promise, not property. What happens depends on your equity plan:
Full forfeiture
Company keeps all. Common in older plans.
Accelerated vesting
All shares vest on death. Common at top FAANG.
Pro-rata vesting
Up to date of death only.
12-month extension
Estate gets extra time to vest/sell.
📖Search your award agreement PDF for "Termination — Death" or ask HR/equity team today.
📈
Stepped-up cost basis — tax gift to your heir
Heir's cost = FMV on date of death. Gains before death: zero tax.
Good news
In both the US and India, inherited stock gets a stepped-up cost basis equal to FMV on date of death. If shares were worth $100 at vest and $400 at death, the heir inherits at $400 — the $300 gain is never taxed.

There is currently no inheritance tax in India.

60-80% in one stock is not a portfolio
Risk

🌏 RNOR Planner
The 2-year tax-free window
most returning NRIs don't know about.
When you return to India after being an NRI, you become "Resident but Not Ordinarily Resident" (RNOR) for 2-3 years. During this window, foreign income — including RSU sales — is NOT taxable in India. This is the most powerful tax planning window available to returning Indians.
🎯
What RNOR means for your RSUs
Foreign income not taxable — this includes RSU sale gains
Big opportunity
Under Section 6(6) of the Income Tax Act, an RNOR is not taxed on income that:
(a) accrues or arises outside India, AND
(b) is not derived from a business/profession set up in India

RSU sale gains on US stocks = foreign income accruing outside India = NOT taxable during RNOR period.

0%
India tax on RSU gains (RNOR)
30%
Slab rate once you become Resident
2–3 yrs
Typical RNOR window
⚠️
RNOR does NOT help with US estate tax
US estate tax follows the asset, not your Indian residency status
Important
RNOR only affects Indian income tax. US estate tax ($60K NRA exemption) still applies during your RNOR period — your US stocks are still US-situs assets regardless of your Indian tax residency status.

💼 Life Events
Changing jobs or facing layoff?
Here's exactly what happens to your RSUs.
Your unvested RSUs are a promise tied to your employment. What happens when that employment ends — voluntarily or not — is one of the most misunderstood parts of equity compensation.
🚪
You resign voluntarily
The most common — and most misunderstood — scenario
Bad leaver
Unvested RSUs: Forfeited immediately on your last day. There is no grace period, no partial credit for time served. If you're 3.5 years into a 4-year vest and resign, you lose the final 6 months of shares.

Vested RSUs already in your account: Yours to keep. They're already shares — employment ending changes nothing.

The cliff trap: If you have an annual cliff (common in India-grant RSUs), resigning 1 day before the cliff date means losing the entire year's vest.
📅Always check your next cliff/vest date before submitting resignation. Waiting even 2 weeks can mean keeping ₹20-30L in shares.
📦
You are laid off
Usually better than resigning — good leaver provisions often apply
Good leaver
Most FAANG equity plans have "good leaver" provisions triggered by involuntary termination (layoff without cause):

✓
Pro-rata accelerated vesting — partial acceleration proportional to service completed (most common at Google, Meta)
✓
Extended exercise window — additional 12-18 months to hold/sell vested shares (depends on plan)
✓
Severance RSU continuation — some companies continue vesting during severance notice period
📖Read your equity award agreement's "Termination — Involuntary" clause BEFORE signing any separation agreement. Separation agreements sometimes ask you to waive equity rights.
⚡
Double-trigger acceleration — the golden scenario
M&A + layoff = full vest. Very common at FAANG acquisitions.
M&A scenario
"Double trigger" means: (1) company is acquired AND (2) you're laid off within 12-24 months of acquisition → all unvested shares vest immediately.

This is standard in most FAANG equity plans. If your company acquires a startup and then lays you off, you typically get full acceleration.

Single trigger (less common) = just the acquisition itself triggers full vest, whether or not you're laid off.
💸
Tax on vest at termination
Accelerated vesting = large perquisite income — often with no TDS
Watch this
If you receive accelerated vesting (good leaver / M&A acceleration), the FMV of all accelerating shares = perquisite income in that financial year — all at once, at your slab rate.

Example: 500 shares accelerate at $180, rate ₹85 = ₹76.5L perquisite income added to your salary in one shot. Final employer TDS may not cover this.

⚠️If acceleration vesting happens late in the financial year, pay self-assessment tax immediately to avoid 234A interest.
🏃
30-day relocation window — the tax time bomb
Layoff in Singapore/US with 30 days to leave — sell or hold?
High pressure
A uniquely stressful scenario: laid off in Singapore or the US, visa tied to employment, 30-60 days to leave the country. Should you sell your vested stock before leaving?

Selling in Singapore before returning to India: Singapore has no capital gains tax. If you sell before becoming India tax resident, the gain may not be taxable in India (especially if you arrive as RNOR).

Selling in the US before returning: US capital gains apply. If LTCG rate (≥12 months), may be lower than India's slab rate as STCG.

💡This decision — sell before or after crossing the border — can be worth ₹10-30L in tax difference. Get specific advice before acting.
